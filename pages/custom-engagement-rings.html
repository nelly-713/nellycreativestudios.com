Custom Engagement Rings in New York

Designed and handmade in Manhattan · Made to order

Nelly designs and hand-makes custom engagement rings in her Manhattan studio. Whether you want an oval diamond engagement ring in 18k blush gold, an old mine cut diamond solitaire, a three stone star sapphire ring or a diamond signet worn as a men's or gender-neutral engagement ring, each ring is made around your stone and your story.

The California Engagement Ring pairs a 3 carat oval diamond set in claws with a delicate 1.3mm half round band in 18k blush gold. It can be customized with pavé on the prongs or any other center stone size and shape. Made-to-order rings take about 2–3 weeks. Looking for a wedding band? Questions

Do you make custom engagement rings in New York?

Yes. Nelly designs and makes custom engagement rings by hand in Manhattan. Start with a consultation through our Bespoke page.

Can you make an oval diamond engagement ring in blush gold?

Yes. The California Engagement Ring is an oval diamond solitaire in 18k blush gold with a delicate half round band, and it can be adapted to your center stone.

How long does a custom engagement ring take?

Made-to-order designs such as The California Engagement Ring take about 2–3 weeks. Fully bespoke commissions vary with the design and are discussed at your consultation.
