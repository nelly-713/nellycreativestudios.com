Gold Granulation Jewelry

22 karat gold granulation · Handmade in Manhattan

Granulation is one of the oldest techniques in goldsmithing. Tiny spheres of gold are arranged and fused onto a gold surface by hand to build texture, pattern and light. Nelly Mendoza-Mendoza works in 22 karat gold granulation in her Manhattan studio, and it has become a signature of Nelly Creative Studios.

This collection brings together our gold granulation jewelry, from the Gold Bee necklace to granulation bracelets and pendants, many set with natural diamonds and gemstones. Every piece is handcrafted from recycled metal, so small variations are part of the work.

Questions

What is gold granulation?

Gold granulation is an ancient goldsmithing technique in which tiny spheres of gold are fused to a gold surface by hand, creating textured designs without large areas of visible solder. Nelly Mendoza-Mendoza trained in the technique and uses it in 22 karat gold.

Where is your granulation jewelry made?

Every piece is handcrafted in Manhattan, New York City, from recycled metal.
