Questions

What is an old mine cut diamond?

An old mine cut is an antique-style, cushion-shaped diamond cut with a high crown, a small table and a large open culet, popular in the 18th and 19th centuries.

How is an old mine cut different from a modern brilliant cut?

A modern brilliant is cut with precise, symmetrical facets for bright white sparkle. An old mine cut has larger facets and a deeper, more rounded look, so it gives broader, softer flashes of light.
