How custom jewelry works: commissioning a piece in New York

November 10, 2026  ·  By Nelly Mendoza-Mendoza

The short answer: a custom piece starts with a conversation about your idea, budget and date. Then comes a design to approve, the choice of stones, the making of the piece by hand, and a final check before it is yours. At Nelly Creative Studios, every commission is designed and made in Manhattan, New York City.

1. The first conversation

Write to the studio with what the piece is for, any images that inspire you, your budget and the date you need it. If you have a stone you would like to use, mention it here.

2. Design

Your idea becomes a design you can see and change before anything is made. This is the moment to ask questions. It is much easier to change a drawing than a finished ring.

3. Stones and metal

Choose the stones and the metal, such as 22k or 18k gold or platinum. These choices set much of the price, so this is where the design is matched to your budget.

4. Making

The piece is made by hand in Manhattan from recycled metal.

5. The final check

Before it is yours, check the fit, that every stone is secure, and how to care for the piece.

What sets the price

  • Metal: type, karat and weight.
  • Stones: type, size and quality, which is usually the largest cost.
  • Labor: how complex the design is. Techniques such as hand granulation take many hours.

How far ahead to start

Start as early as you can, and tell the studio your date in the first message. For the 2026 holidays, the last day to order a custom or bespoke piece is December 5.

Start a commission, or see custom engagement rings.

Questions

How much does custom jewelry cost?
It depends on the metal, the stones and the complexity of the design. Share your budget in the first conversation and the design is matched to it.

How long does a custom piece take?
It depends on the design and on finding the stones. Give the studio your date at the start. For the 2026 holidays, order custom pieces by December 5.

Who makes custom jewelry in New York?
Nelly Creative Studios designs and makes custom jewelry and custom engagement rings by hand in Manhattan, New York City.

Written by Nelly Mendoza-Mendoza, fine jewelry designer in New York City, GIA Gemology Graduate and four years in the Tiffany & Co. Innovation and Design Workshop. About Nelly.
