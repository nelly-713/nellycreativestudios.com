How to choose huggie earrings

November 10, 2026  ·  By Nelly Mendoza-Mendoza

The short answer: huggie earrings are small, thick hoops that sit close to the earlobe. Choose them by size, closure, metal and stones: smaller huggies sit snug against the lobe, a hinged snap closure should click firmly shut, and the harder the stone, the better it wears every day.

Size

Huggies are measured by their inner diameter. The smaller the diameter, the closer the hoop hugs the lobe. For a second or third piercing higher on the ear, go smaller. For a single pair worn alone, a slightly larger huggie shows more of the design.

Closure

Most huggies use a hinged snap: a short post on one side that clicks into the other. Open and close it a few times. It should feel secure, with no wobble.

Metal

Match the metal she already wears most: yellow gold, white metal such as platinum, or a mix. Gold huggies are easy to wear every day and stack well with other earrings.

Gemstones

Hardness, on the Mohs scale, tells you how well a stone stands up to daily wear.

  • Most durable: diamond (10), aquamarine (7.5 to 8) and amethyst (7). Good for every day.
  • Wear with some care: moonstone (6 to 6.5), opal (5.5 to 6.5), turquoise (5 to 6) and lapis lazuli (5 to 5.5). Take them off for the gym, cleaning and sleep.
  • Softest: pearl (2.5 to 4.5). Put pearls on last, after perfume and lotion, and wipe them after wearing.

Why huggies make an easy gift

There is no ring size to guess. If she has pierced ears, a pair of huggies fits. They suit everyday wear, so they are worn rather than kept in a drawer.

Questions

What is the difference between huggies and hoops?
Huggies are small, thicker hoops that sit close to the earlobe. Regular hoops are larger and hang lower.

Can you sleep in huggie earrings?
Many people do, because huggies sit close to the ear. Take off pieces set with softer stones such as opal, turquoise or pearl, to protect them.
