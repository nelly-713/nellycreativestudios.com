Buying Guide

Jewelry gifts for your wife: a jeweler's guide

November 10, 2026  ·  By Nelly Mendoza-Mendoza

The short answer: choose by what she already wears, not by what is in fashion. Look at the jewelry she reaches for every day (the metal color, the size, earrings or necklaces) and pick a piece that fits beside it. Earrings and necklaces are the safest choices, because there is no ring size to guess.

Start with her jewelry box

  • Metal: does she wear yellow gold, white metals such as platinum, or both?
  • Scale: small and delicate, or bold?
  • What she wears most: if her earrings change daily but her necklace never does, earrings are the better gift.
  • Pierced ears: check before buying earrings.

Earrings or a necklace?

Earrings are worn close to the face and suit almost everyone. Huggie earrings are a good first choice because they are easy to wear every day. Necklaces are worn close to the heart and suit pendants with meaning. An 18-inch chain is the most common length. A 16-inch chain sits near the collarbone.

Gifts by occasion

  • Anniversary or milestone: a piece with a story, such as an old mine cut diamond or 22k gold granulation. See gifts for a milestone.
  • Holiday or birthday: something she can wear every day. See everyday pieces.
  • The wife who has everything: a one of a kind piece with a rare stone she won't own already. See gifts for the collector.
  • Under $1,000: see gifts under $1,000.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Guessing a ring size. If you want a ring, consider a custom piece made to her size after the reveal.
  • Buying for a trend she doesn't follow.
  • Choosing size over quality. A smaller, well-made piece is worn more than a large one that isn't.

Questions

What is a good jewelry gift for a wife?
Earrings or a necklace in the metal she already wears. Huggie earrings are an easy everyday choice, and a pendant suits a gift with meaning.

What jewelry do you give a wife who has everything?
Something one of a kind: a handmade piece with a rare stone or an old technique such as gold granulation, which she won't already own.
